      Advanced
      

        
        
        

          

            
            
            
Splits speech from the rest so replacing the voices
              doesn't wipe the soundtrack.

          

          

            
            
            
Only used when “Who's speaking?” is set to several
              people. Working it out automatically is the least reliable part of
              the chain, so if you know the answer, saying so here is better.

          

          

            
            
            
Only applies when the soundtrack was separated and the
              original audio is being replaced.

          

          

            
            
          

          

            
            
            
Cloning keeps the speaker's identity, but
              carries their accent into English.

          

        

        

          

            
            
            
For videos YouTube refuses to send to a signed-out
              request. Borrows the session from a browser you're already signed
              into on this Mac. Nothing is uploaded; the cookies are only sent to
              the site the video is on.

          

          

            
            
            
The picture is copied, never re-encoded, so this
              decides the download size. On an hour-and-a-half tutorial, 1080p is
              around 1.8 GB and 720p around 730 MB — and 720p is plenty for
              following along.

          

          

            
            
          

          

            
            
            
Used only by “Keep quietly underneath”.

          

          

            
            
            
Fast dialogue arrives as many very short lines with no
              gap between them, and each has to be squeezed to fit. Joining them
              gives the translation room. Material with real pauses is unaffected.

          

          

            
            
            
How much a line may be sped up to fit the gap the
              original speaker left. Past about 1.6x it starts to sound hurried;
              beyond the limit the line runs on and later pauses absorb it.
